## Discovery and presentation rules

- Background and texture assets are people-free, visually quiet enough to sit behind content, and tagged `background-safe`.
- Product assets preserve a centered focal point and render with `contain` so the interface or physical product is never cropped into an accidental texture.
- Editorial and campaign media can be more expressive, but cannot be silently substituted for a background or product record.
- Each built-in identity has twelve distinct discovery slots: overview, editorial, detail, atmosphere, campaign, interface, motion, hero, workflow, system, material, and signal.
- Original Glyphfield diagrams are used when an official site does not provide a suitable people-free background or an accurate product-system explanation.

Do not export or redistribute a research-only asset as a standalone stock asset. Replace research-only records with organization-owned or separately licensed files before commercial reuse.
