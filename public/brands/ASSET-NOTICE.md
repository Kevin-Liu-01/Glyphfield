# Brand research assets

Glyphfield's source code is MIT-licensed. Third-party brand marks, product media, photography, and press-kit files under `public/brands` are not relicensed by Glyphfield.

The application records the source owner, source URL, intended use, and redistribution status for every research asset. Unless an individual record says `original`, rights remain with the named owner and the owner's terms apply.

- Ramp media comes from the [official Ramp press kit](https://ramp.com/press). Ramp describes the press-kit media as royalty-free for web and print.
- Cloudflare media comes from the [official Cloudflare press kit](https://www.cloudflare.com/press/press-kit/).
- Stripe media comes from [Stripe newsroom media assets](https://stripe.com/newsroom/information).
- Tailwind CSS media comes from the [official Tailwind CSS site and brand page](https://tailwindcss.com/brand).
- Mintlify media comes from the [official Mintlify site](https://www.mintlify.com/).
- Vite+ media comes from the [official Vite+ site](https://viteplus.dev/).
- Basement and Speakeasy project media comes from the [official Basement Studio site](https://basement.studio/).
- General Translation diagrams in `public/brands/gt/assets` are original Glyphfield interpretations informed by the [official General Translation site](https://generaltranslation.com/en-US/). Additional files under `public/brands/gt/library` are native GT product and onboarding artwork and remain subject to GT's source terms.

## Discovery and presentation rules

- Background and texture assets are people-free, visually quiet enough to sit behind content, and tagged `background-safe`.
- Product assets preserve a centered focal point and render with `contain` so the interface or physical product is never cropped into an accidental texture.
- Editorial and campaign media can be more expressive, but cannot be silently substituted for a background or product record.
- Each built-in identity has twelve distinct discovery slots: overview, editorial, detail, atmosphere, campaign, interface, motion, hero, workflow, system, material, and signal.
- Original Glyphfield diagrams are used when an official site does not provide a suitable people-free background or an accurate product-system explanation.

Do not export or redistribute a research-only asset as a standalone stock asset. Replace research-only records with organization-owned or separately licensed files before commercial reuse.
