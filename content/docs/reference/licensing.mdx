Glyphfield is released under the [MIT License](https://github.com/Kevin-Liu-01/Glyphfield/blob/main/LICENSE). The license covers Glyphfield's source code and its original artwork, including the Starter identity and the artwork under `public/templates`. It does not cover the assets bundled with the reference identities, the reference-brand font files, or third-party code and packages, which keep their own terms.

## What keeps its own terms

| Material | Location | Terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reference brand marks, product media, photography, and press-kit files | `public/brands/<brand>` | The owner's terms. Each asset record stores its owner, source URL, intended use, and redistribution status. | General Translation artifact pictures | `public/brands/gt/library` | Each keeps its original's license; see the table below |
| Reference-brand font files | `public/fonts/brands` | Each typeface keeps its owner's or foundry's terms. See [Reference brand webfonts](https://github.com/Kevin-Liu-01/Glyphfield/blob/main/public/fonts/brands/README.md) |

The reference identities (Basement, General Translation, Ramp, Mintlify, Tailwind CSS, Vite+, Cloudflare, Stripe) are included for design study and interoperability. Their inclusion does not imply endorsement, and the MIT license grants no rights to third-party trademarks. Research-only assets must not be exported or redistributed as standalone stock assets; replace them with owned or licensed files before commercial use.

## Exported artifacts

An export contains whatever the composition uses. A file made with a reference identity can contain that brand's marks, pictures, and fonts, and those keep their owners' terms. A file made with your own identity and Glyphfield's original artwork carries no Glyphfield restriction. Lottie files from LottieFiles keep their individual licenses; check each one before use.
