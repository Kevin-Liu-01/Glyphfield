The partner lockup is one layer holding the active brand and any number of partners. Partners are logos from the identity's proof assets, other built-in identities, an uploaded file, or a written name. Without a real partner on file the template uses a written **Partner** placeholder rather than an invented brand.

Optical sizing balances partners on the ink they show. Bundled logos carry a measured ink box, so padding inside a logo file does not make that partner smaller.

## Content controls

- **Title**, **GT headline font**, and **GT headline weight** edit the headline. A headline in the form "Brand × Partner" follows the partner you choose.
- **Partner**, **Partner treatment** (Use logo or Write name), **Lockup name**, and **Replace partner logo** set the first partner.
- **Partner headline font**, **Upload partner headline font**, and **Partner headline weight** style the partner's name in the headline and in a written lockup.
- **Lockup spacing** sets the gap around each separator.
- In the **Lockup** section, **Add partner** and **Add written partner** add more partners; each extra partner has its own artwork picker, upload, and remove button.

## Alignment rules

- **Sizing**: **Optical balance** gives every mark the same visual area, so a square mark and a long wordmark carry equal weight; **Equal height** and **Equal width** are also available.
- **Separator**: ×, +, /, a thin rule, or none.
- **Alignment** (start, center, end) places the lockup in its box, and **Vertical** aligns marks on their centers, tops, or baselines.
- **Logo color**: one color (marks recolored to the text color) or original colors.

The lockup scales down to fit its box. Layouts: **Lockup**, **Centered**, **Hero lockup**, and **Announcement**.
