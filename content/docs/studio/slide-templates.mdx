Slide templates is a deck. A new deck has five slides built from the identity: a title slide, an agenda from its strategy pillars, its proof figures as metrics (or a statement when it has none), a quote in its voice, and a closing slide with its website.

## Slides

The **Slides** section lists every slide with a live thumbnail. Click a slide to edit it. **Add slide** inserts a slide after the current one in the chosen **New slide layout**; **Duplicate slide**, **Move slide up**, **Move slide down**, and **Delete slide** act on the current slide. Drag a thumbnail to reorder, or focus it and press Alt with an arrow key.

Each slide has its own layout. The **Slide library** applies one of 14 layouts to the current slide:

1. Title
2. Section
3. Agenda
4. Split
5. Metrics
6. Quote
7. Timeline
8. Statement
9. Comparison
10. Process
11. Chart
12. Team
13. Image
14. Closing

Switching layouts keeps the slide's text: the headline, list items, metrics, and labels move into the new layout, and anything the new layout has no place for comes back when you switch again.

## Content controls

- **Title**, **Typography role**, and **Font weight** edit the current slide's headline.
- **Body or list · one item per line** sets the list items for agenda, timeline, process, comparison, and team slides.
- **Speaker notes** are stored on the slide.
- **Surface** chooses **Base white**, **Base dark**, **Grid**, or **Noise** for the current slide, and **Add background image** places an image behind it.

Footers can show slide numbers with `{page}` and `{pages}` in any text layer, for example `{page} / {pages}`.

## Formats and export

Formats: 16:9 (1600 × 900), 4:3 (1600 × 1200), and Square (1200 × 1200); a format change re-flows every slide. **Export PNG** renders the current slide. **More exports → Export PDF deck** renders every slide into one PDF with lossless page images.

## Browser actions

| Action | Use |
|---|---|
| `slides.export.png` / `slides.export.svg` | Export the current slide |
| `slides.export.pdf` | Export every slide as one PDF |
| `slides.slide.add` / `slides.slide.duplicate` / `slides.slide.move` / `slides.slide.remove` / `slides.slide.select` | Deck management |
| `slides.document.describe` | Format, layouts, slides, and layers |
| `slides.layer.add` / `slides.layer.update` / `slides.layer.remove` / `slides.layer.duplicate` | Layer edits |
| `slides.layer.reorder` / `slides.layer.select` / `slides.layer.align` | Stack, selection, and alignment |
| `slides.format.set` / `slides.layout.apply` / `slides.template.reset` | Format, layout (`pageId` optional), and template |
| `slides.history.undo` / `slides.history.redo` | History |
