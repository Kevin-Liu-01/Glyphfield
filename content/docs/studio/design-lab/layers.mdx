Every element in Design Lab, the layered composition tool, is a layer in one ordered stack. The layer dock and the **Layers** section of the inspector list layers front to back. In source, `page.elementIds` stores the same order back to front.

## Layer kinds

| Kind | Add with | Default box | Inspector controls |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Text | **Text** or T | 72% × 25% of the artboard | Copy, font, size, weight, styles, color, alignment, wrapping, line height, tracking, text effect, outline, shadow, opacity |
| Shader | **Shader**, S, or a library card | The full artboard | Colors, palette, material settings, zoom, frame position and size, opacity, blend mode ([Shaders and time](/docs/studio/design-lab/shaders)) |
| Effect | **Effect** or E | The full artboard | Converter type or image effect, effect settings, opacity ([Effect layers](/docs/studio/design-lab/effects)) |
| Mark | **Mark** or M | 42% × 32% | Mark asset, color, appearance, opacity |
| Sticker | **Sticker** or K | 34% × 38% | Image, crop, surface finish, finish settings, appearance, opacity |
| Image | **Image** or I | 34% × 38% | Image, crop, focus, zoom, appearance, opacity |

I and K open the image import dialog, which adds the chosen files as images or stickers. New text, text stickers, and recolorable marks start black in light mode and white in dark mode. Text outlines start with the opposite color. These colors are stored on the layer when it is created. Changing the Studio theme, duplicating a layer, or reopening a project does not recolor existing layers. Imported images and shader palettes keep their own colors.

A new layer is selected when it is added.

## Select, move, and resize

- Click a layer on the canvas or its card in the dock to select it. Shift-click or ⌘/Ctrl-click a layer on the canvas to add it to the selection.
- Drag to move. While dragging, the layer snaps within 6 screen pixels to the artboard's edges, center, and rule-of-thirds lines, and to the edges and centers of other layers. A rotated layer snaps by the area it covers. On the open canvas, layers snap to edges and centers only.
- Drag a selection handle to resize. Shader layers can also be sized with **Frame width** and **Frame height** (2% to 300% of the artboard) and placed with **Horizontal position** and **Vertical position**.
- Press the arrow keys to nudge by 1 px, or by 10 px with Shift.

Text and image boxes do not resize to fit their contents. Text that overflows a narrow box stays visible in the preview and in the export.

### Edit several layers at once

With more than one layer selected, the inspector shows the last layer you selected and its meta reads the number of layers. A change in the inspector applies to every selected layer that has that setting:

- Layers of the same kind take every change: font, color, size, weight, styles, line height, tracking, text effect, outline, shadow, appearance, sticker finish and box width.
- Text, marks and images all take **Layer opacity**, and marks and images share appearance.
- **Layer rotation** turns each selected layer about its own center.
- Shader settings, palettes, **Reset shader**, a material picked from the library and **Remove shader from layer** reach every selected shader layer and every selected layer with a material. A layer without a material only gains one when you pick a material.
- On shader layers, **Frame width** and **Frame height** apply to each selected shader layer, and the position fields move the selection together.

Text content, image crop, a mark's source and each layer's position stay per layer. A font size is set on each text box from its own box, so boxes keep their positions. When part of a text box is selected for formatting, changes apply only to those characters.

## Rotate layers

Text, mark, image, sticker, and shader layers rotate about their centers.

- Drag the round handle below a selected layer to rotate it. Hold Shift to rotate in 15° steps.
- **Layer rotation** in the inspector sets the angle in degrees, clockwise. Angles are stored in the range −180° to 180°, so 270 is stored as −90.
- A rotated layer resizes along its own sides.
- **Reset mark position**, **Reset image position**, **Reset text box**, and **Fit shader to canvas** also reset the rotation.
- Align, distribute, and **Frame selection** use the area a rotated layer covers.
- Copy, paste, and duplicate keep the rotation. Copy style does not copy it.
- The **Rotation** slider in a shader's **Advanced** section turns the material inside the layer. **Layer rotation** turns the layer itself.

Effect layers cover the whole artboard and do not rotate.

`design.layers.rotate` takes `{ layerIds: string[], rotation: number }`. Each listed unlocked layer turns to `rotation` degrees clockwise about its own center. Locked layers are skipped, and the action fails when every listed layer is locked. `0` removes the rotation. The action returns `null` after the change commits.

## Layer dock and menus

Each dock card shows a live thumbnail, the layer name, its position in the stack, and its kind. The card buttons are **Duplicate**, **Move forward**, **Move backward**, **Hide layer** or **Show layer**, **Lock layer** or **Unlock layer**, and **Delete**. Effect layer cards have no lock button. Converter thumbnails draw the converter's real algorithm on a sample, and image effect thumbnails show the effect's library preview.

Right-click a layer on the canvas, or press Shift + F10, for the selection menu:

- **Move to canvas** and the named artboards, or **Frame selection** for loose layers
- **Crop image** (or **Finish cropping**) and **Reset crop** for images and stickers
- **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste**, **Duplicate**, **Select all layers**
- **Copy style**, **Paste style**
- **Group selection**, **Ungroup selection**
- **Bring forward**, **Bring to front**, **Send backward**, **Send to back**
- **Align selection** (two or more layers) and **Align to artboard**
- **Lock selection**, **Selection visible** or **Selection hidden**, **Delete selection**

Right-click a dock card or a row in the inspector's **Layers** list for the layer menu. It has **Copy layer**, **Cut layer**, **Paste**, **Duplicate layer**, **Copy style**, **Paste style**, **Layer visible** or **Layer hidden**, **Lock layer** or **Unlock layer**, the four order commands, and **Delete layer**. Effect layers have no style or lock items.

Multi-selection and groups move related layers as one unit. ⌘/Ctrl + G groups the selection, and Shift + ⌘/Ctrl + G ungroups it. Each layer in a group keeps its own settings.

**Undo** and **Redo** sit in the canvas toolbar, and **Action history** lists recent edits. ⌘/Ctrl + Z and Shift + ⌘/Ctrl + Z work while the canvas has focus or the pointer is over it. Fields in the inspector keep their own text undo. Named checkpoints in **Saved designs** are separate from the edit history. The full shortcut list is on the [Design Lab](/docs/studio/design-lab#keyboard-shortcuts) page.

## Clipboard, paste, and drop

⌘/Ctrl + C and ⌘/Ctrl + V copy and paste layers or a whole artboard, including their assets and captured shader frames. ⌘/Ctrl + X copies the selected layers and deletes them. Pasting image files from the system clipboard, or dropping image files onto an artboard or the canvas, opens the image import for those files. The accepted image types are PNG, JPG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, BMP, and SVG, up to 4,000,000 bytes each.

## Text

Text is a canvas layer with its own box. New text starts at 48 px.

| Control | Range or values |
| --- | --- |
| Text content | Any text. Edit it in the inspector, or click the text on the canvas and type |
| Font | A brand role (Display, Body, Accent, Code), which follows the identity, or one font: the brand's font files and every font Glyphfield ships, Katagami included |
| **Text size in pixels** and **Text size** | 1–2048 artboard pixels in the number field. The slider reaches 512 |
| **Font weight** | The supported range of the selected font |
| Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough | Toggles. Underline and strikethrough combine |
| Alignment | Left, center, right |
| Wrapping | **Wrap** or **Single line** |
| Text box width | 0.25–3 × the default box |
| Line height | 0.7–1.8 |
| Tracking | −0.12 to 0.2 em |
| Text effect | Solid, Bayer sweep, Halftone, Scan lines, Gradient ink, with **Effect strength**, **Pattern scale**, and **Effect angle** |
| Outline | Color and width 0.5–12 px |
| Shadow | Color, blur 0–64 px, X and Y offset ±48 px, opacity |
| Layer opacity | 0–100% |

Setting a pixel size keeps the box position and dimensions and resets the separate layer scale to 1. Older designs without a pixel size keep their height-relative size until the size is edited.

### Format part of a text box

Select words or lines on the canvas or in the inspector text field. The inspector shows **Selected text · N characters**. Font, size, weight, color, italic, underline, strikethrough, and tracking then apply only to those characters. **Whole text box** returns to formatting the whole box. A selection that spans different styles shows **Mixed formatting**. Alignment, wrapping, line height, opacity, outline, shadow, text effect, and shader fill always apply to the whole box.

While editing with a range of text selected, ⌘/Ctrl + B, ⌘/Ctrl + I, and ⌘/Ctrl + U toggle bold, italic, and underline. Formatting follows the characters as text is inserted or deleted. Plain-text paste takes the formatting at the insertion point. Pasted HTML is not imported.

In source, a text element stores its plain `value` and optional ordered `runs`: `{ start, end, style }` with UTF-16 offsets and an exclusive end. Unset style fields inherit from the layer. Run `fontSize` is in authored pixels before layer scale. A `fontId` on the layer or a run names one font file and wins over `fontRole`. Setting either one replaces the other.

`design.text.format` applies run styling through the Browser API (`window.glyphfield.studio`):

```js
await window.glyphfield.studio.invoke('design.text.format', {
  layerId: 'text-1',
  start: 0,
  end: 5,
  style: { color: '#2F5CE0', weight: 600 },
});
```

`style` accepts `color`, `fontRole` (`Display`, `Body`, `Accent`, `Code`), `fontId` (a brand font id or a shipped one such as `katagami-sans-variable`), `fontSize`, `weight`, `fontStyle` (`normal`, `italic`), `underline`, `strikethrough`, and `tracking`. The range must be non-empty and inside the text. The action returns `null`. Read `element.data.runs` from `readSource()` to confirm the change.

### Text rendering options

- A solid identity color
- A live shader clipped to the glyphs (choose a material while the text layer is selected)
- A text effect preset
- A converter or image effect from an Effect layer above it
- A sticker with a cut border and a finish
- An outline and a shadow

## Images and crop

Images come from local files or the identity asset library. Double-click an image or sticker, or choose **Crop image** from its menu, to enter crop mode. The frame stays in place, and the parts of the source outside it are dimmed.

- Drag the source to reposition it. Hold Shift to lock the drag to one axis.
- Drag an edge or corner of the frame to change the crop area.
- Press the arrow keys to move the source.
- Double-click again, press Enter or Escape, or choose **Done** to finish.

The image inspector has **Crop to frame**. With it on, the inspector adds **Edit crop on canvas**, **Fill frame**, **Fit image**, **Original ratio**, **Crop aspect** (Freeform, Square · 1:1, Landscape · 4:3, Widescreen · 16:9, Portrait · 3:4, Story · 9:16), **Crop zoom**, **Horizontal focus**, **Vertical focus**, and **Reset crop**. Cropping is non-destructive. Duplicate, undo, autosave, saved designs, project files, source, and export all keep the crop.

The image and sticker pickers list saved brand assets. Each asset has a delete action that asks for confirmation and removes only the library copy. Layers already placed keep their embedded image. If browser storage is full, delete unused library assets and import again.

## Stickers

A sticker is an image layer with a cut border and a physical finish. **Surface finish** has 13 presets: Holo vinyl, Prismatic, Mirror chrome, Brushed metal, Pearl laminate, Glitter flake, Retroreflective, Clear frost, Soft touch, Spot gloss, Epoxy dome, Embossed foil, and Precision metal inset. **Shine**, **Texture**, **Relief**, **Glint angle**, and **Depth** adjust the finish.

**Die-cut outline** sets the border's color and width (up to 24 px). An inner separation edge keeps white artwork visible on a white border. A sticker needs no background. Export it over the artboard color or combine it with a shader layer.

## Marks

A mark layer places a logo from the active identity: the dark or light mark, the wordmark, or another logo asset. Its inspector has **Mark color**, opacity, the appearance controls, **SVG conversion & mark library**, and **Reset mark position**.

**SVG conversion & mark library** adds local SVG, PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, AVIF, or BMP files smaller than 25 MB as marks. It sanitizes SVG input, converts each file to a transparent PNG at 1024, 2048, or 4096 px (**Converted asset size**), and keeps the original and the PNG in IndexedDB in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

## Appearance

Marks, images, and stickers share these controls:

| Control | Mark label | Image and sticker label | Settings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Invert | **Invert logo color** | **Invert image colors** | On or off |
| Dither | **Dither logo** | **Dither image** | **Dither amount** 0–100%, **Cell size** 2–18 px, **Dither direction** 0°–360° |
| Outline | **Outline** | **Outline**, or **Die-cut outline** on stickers | Color with opacity, **Outline width** 0.5–12 px (24 px on stickers) |
| Shadow | **Logo shadow** | **Image shadow** or **Sticker shadow** | Color with opacity, **Blur** 0–64 px, **Horizontal offset** and **Vertical offset** ±48 px |

The outline follows the alpha shape of the SVG or transparent image.

## Align and distribute

Select two or more layers and open the selection menu. **Align selection** aligns the layers to the bounds of the whole selection: **Align left edges**, **Align horizontal centers**, **Align right edges**, **Align top edges**, **Align vertical centers**, and **Align bottom edges**. **Distribute horizontally** and **Distribute vertically** need three or more units. The outermost units stay in place, and every neighboring pair gets the same gap. **Align to artboard** aligns the selection to the artboard's edges and centers with **Align left**, **Center horizontally**, **Align right**, **Align top**, **Center vertically**, and **Align bottom**.

A fully selected group moves as one unit. Locked layers are skipped. Effect layers are not arranged.

`design.layers.arrange` runs the same operations:

| Input | Values |
| --- | --- |
| `arrangement` | `left`, `horizontal-center`, `right`, `top`, `vertical-center`, `bottom`, `distribute-horizontal`, `distribute-vertical` |
| `layerIds` | Optional. Omit it, or pass an empty array, to use the current selection |

Alignment needs two units and distribution needs three. The action returns `null` after the change commits.

## Lock layers

Lock a layer from its dock card, the **Layers** list in the inspector, either menu, or Shift + ⌘/Ctrl + L. Shift + ⌘/Ctrl + L locks the targets if any of them is unlocked, and unlocks them otherwise.

A locked layer stays visible and exports normally. It ignores canvas clicks, drags, resize handles, on-canvas text editing, and nudges, and it is left out of the canvas selection and **Select all layers**. Its inspector controls stay available. Locks are saved as `element.locked` in source, autosave, saved designs, and project files. Effect layers cannot be locked.

`design.layers.lock` takes `{ layerIds: string[], locked: boolean }` and returns `null`.

## Copy and paste style

**Copy style** (Option/Alt + ⌘/Ctrl + C) takes the selected layer's appearance. **Paste style** (Option/Alt + ⌘/Ctrl + V) applies it to the selected unlocked layers. Content, position, size, rotation, name, visibility, and lock state stay unchanged.

| Source layer | Style that is copied |
| --- | --- |
| Text | Font role, size, weight, italic, underline, strikethrough, tracking, line height, alignment, wrapping, color, outline, shadow, text effect, opacity. Pasting clears inline run overrides for those properties |
| Mark | Appearance, color, opacity |
| Image | Appearance, opacity |
| Sticker | Appearance, opacity, finish |
| Shader, or a layer with a material fill | The material, which fills content targets |

`design.layers.copyStyle` takes `{ layerId }`. `design.layers.pasteStyle` takes `{ layerIds }` and skips locked layers. It fails with "Copy a style first, then paste it onto unlocked layers." when no style was copied or every target is locked. Both actions return `null`. Effect layers cannot be style sources or targets.

## Opacity and blending

Every layer has its own opacity. Shader layers also have a blend mode: Normal, Screen, Overlay, or Multiply. A material applied to a text, mark, or image layer has its own opacity and blend mode in addition to the layer opacity. **Remove shader from layer** takes the material off a content layer.
