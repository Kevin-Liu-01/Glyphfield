Blog cover starts from one of the identity's voice phrases as the headline. Layouts: **Editorial** (mark and name above a headline anchored to the foot of the page), **Feature image** (copy beside a library image or the brand device), **Statement**, **Centered**, and **Index** (ruled masthead and footer).

## Content controls

- **Title**, **Typography role**, and **Font weight** edit the headline.
- **Surface** chooses **Base white**, **Base dark**, **Grid**, or **Noise**. Grid and noise add a texture layer you can restyle or remove.
- **Add background image** and **Brand background asset** place a full-bleed image behind the copy; **Background image opacity** controls it.

Every layer is editable on the canvas; see [Expression tools](/docs/studio/templates). Formats are the same as OpenGraph.
